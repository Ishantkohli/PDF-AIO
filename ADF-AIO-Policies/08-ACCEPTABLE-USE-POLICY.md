## Prohibited Conduct

You agree not to use **PDF (AIO)** to:

1. **Unlawful or Fraudulent Activity:** Forge signatures, falsify official contracts, alter legal records unlawfully, or violate any applicable local, national, or international law.
2. **Intellectual Property Infringement:** Strip copyright notices, redistribute copyrighted PDF materials unlawfully, or repackage **PDF (AIO)** proprietary source code or branding without written permission.
3. **Malicious Distribution:** Embed, generate, or share malicious payloads or harmful files disguised as PDFs, DOCX, or TXT exports.
4. **Abuse of Update or Feedback Channels:** Spam or automate excessive requests against the configured GitHub Releases endpoint or abuse the developer's feedback/email channels.
5. **Misrepresentation:** Present unauthorized builds or modified forks as official releases from Ishant Kohli.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Because PDF (AIO) processes documents strictly on-device without centralized user accounts, we do not monitor your private documents. If you encounter unauthorized repackaged distributions, brand impersonation, or security abuse involving the official GitHub repository or app links, report details to **ishantkohlikohli@gmail.com**.
