## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies may store preferences, session data, identifiers, or other information in a browser or device.

## 2. Categories

The app should classify technologies according to their actual purpose:

- **Strictly necessary:** required for security, navigation, or a feature the user requests.
- **Preferences:** remember choices such as theme or language.
- **Analytics/performance:** measure usage or diagnose performance.
- **Advertising/marketing:** measure or personalize advertising, if applicable.
- **Third-party embeds:** may be set or accessed by external providers when embedded content loads.

These are categories, not a claim that every category is currently used.
