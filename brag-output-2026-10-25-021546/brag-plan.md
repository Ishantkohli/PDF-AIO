# ADF AIO — Launch Video Plan

## Planning Rubric
1. **What is it?**
   ADF AIO is an ultra-fast, modern, all-in-one Android PDF powerhouse with multi-format document conversion, on-device document scanning, digital signatures, watermark studio, and high-refresh-rate display optimization.
2. **Who is it for?**
   Professionals, students, and power users who need instant document reading, conversion, and editing without ads, bloat, or slow cloud uploads.
3. **What sets it apart?**
   Hardware-accelerated performance (unlocked 120Hz/165Hz display rates), on-device conversion engine (Images to PDF, PDF to Images, Word to PDF), and sleek cyberpunk neon visual identity.
4. **Most impressive claim?**
   Every document tool you need in a single dark-mode, neon-accented app running silky smooth at up to 165 FPS.
5. **Visual hook:**
   Neon holographic banner animation flashing into an ultra-modern mobile app interface with real UI widgets.
6. **Real UI shown:**
   ADF AIO neon header, Document Conversion Hub actions (Images → PDF, PDF → Images, Word → PDF), and hardware FPS unlocker.
7. **Tone:**
   `default` / `cinematic-modern`: High-energy, crisp typography, electric cyan and violet glow, dynamic motion.
8. **Share caption:**
   Meet ADF AIO — your ultimate all-in-one document powerhouse with ultra-fast conversions, sleek neon UI, and hardware-accelerated smoothness.
